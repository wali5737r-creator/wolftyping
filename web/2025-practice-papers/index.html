About the 2025 set

Papers are numbered 162 to 217. Paper 218 is not included — it was not in the source collection. Paper 209 has 89 questions and paper 217 has 92; the first question of paper 210 is not included; paper 201 repeats the questions of paper 197.

Every paper runs as a timed test — choose 60, 90 or 120 minutes (90 is the default) — with a warning in the last five minutes, automatic submission when time ends, and a final score with correct, incorrect and skipped review.
