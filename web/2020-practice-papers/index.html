About the 2020 set

Papers are numbered 32 to 60. Papers 39 and 46 carry a 2021 exam date in their printed header and are grouped here under 2020, as in the contents listing. A small number of printed questions could not be read or verified and were left out rather than guessed.

Every paper runs as a timed test — choose 60, 90 or 120 minutes (90 is the default) — with a warning in the last five minutes, automatic submission when time ends, and a final score with correct, incorrect and skipped review. Current-affairs questions carry a latest-update note saying who holds the office or post now.
