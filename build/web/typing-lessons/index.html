How the lessons work

Each lesson teaches a small set of keys and then lets you practise them until they feel natural. You start on the home row and work outward, stage by stage, so you never have to memorise the whole keyboard at once. Your lesson progress is saved on your own device, and you can repeat any lesson as often as you like.

English lessons — 46 lessons in 8 stages

  1. Stage 1 · Home Row — anchor keys F and J, then D and K, S and L, A and semicolon.
  2. Stage 2 · Top Row — the keys above the home row, finger by finger.
  3. Stage 3 · Bottom Row — the keys below the home row, finger by finger.
  4. Stage 4 · Shift & Punctuation — capital letters and punctuation marks.
  5. Stage 5 · Number Row — the number keys.
  6. Stage 6 · Symbols — symbols and special characters.
  7. Stage 7 · Words & Sentences — putting the keys together into real words and sentences.
  8. Stage 8 · Speed & Accuracy — timed practice to build speed without losing accuracy.

Urdu lessons — 42 lessons in 7 stages

Urdu lessons follow the same step-by-step path — home row, top row, bottom row, shift and punctuation, number row, words and sentences, then speed and accuracy — using an InPage-style phonetic key map, so the Latin key you press decides the Urdu letter. Urdu text is shown in Nastaleeq script.

Weak-key drills — fix the keys you keep missing

WolfTyping records the keys you get wrong during tests and practice. The weak-keys drill builds a practice session from exactly those keys, so your practice time goes where it helps most instead of repeating keys you already know.

Practise with your own paragraph

When you are ready to move beyond lessons, paste any paragraph in My Paragraphs and take a typing test with it — your own text, a job description, or anything you type often. Then measure yourself with a timed English typing test or Urdu typing test.

Free, no account. All lessons are free, and your progress stays on your own device. If you are preparing for a test, the timed PPSC practice mock test with 237 timed practice papers is the next step after the lessons.

Frequently asked questions

How do typing lessons work for beginners?

Typing lessons on WolfTyping walk you through keys step by step, and practice drills focus on the keys you get wrong most, so you improve where you need it.

Do I need any typing experience to start the lessons?

No. The lessons start from the home row and teach the keys finger by finger, so a complete beginner can start at Stage 1 and work through the stages in order.

What is a weak-keys drill?

A weak-keys drill is a practice session built from the keys you get wrong most. WolfTyping records your mistakes during tests and practice, then offers drills that focus on those keys until they improve.

Can I practise typing with my own paragraph?

Yes. You can paste your own paragraph in My Paragraphs and start a typing test with it.

Are the typing lessons free?

Yes. All typing lessons on WolfTyping are free, and you do not need an account to use them.
