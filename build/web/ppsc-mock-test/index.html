How the mock test works

  • 100 questions, one page. All questions appear together on one scrollable page with a question palette, so you can jump to any question and see which ones you have answered.
  • Choose your time: 60, 90 or 120 minutes. The default is 90 minutes. A warning appears in the last five minutes, and the test submits automatically when time ends.
  • Full test or one subject. Take the full 100-question test, or a shorter subject-wise test in English, General Knowledge, Pakistan Studies, Islamiat, Mathematics or Everyday Science.
  • Score and review. The result shows your score and correct, incorrect and skipped counts, with a per-question review of every answer.
  • Negative marking is optional and off by default. Turn it on before starting if you want it; skipped questions never deduct, and your score never goes below zero.
  • Free, no account. Take the test without creating an account; your results stay on your own device.

What is in the question bank

The built-in practice bank contains 600 practice sample questions — 100 for each subject. A full practice test draws a balanced 100 questions across the six subjects, and each subject test uses that subject's 100 questions. The order of the options is shuffled on every run, so the correct answer is never in the same place twice — but the questions and answers themselves do not change.
