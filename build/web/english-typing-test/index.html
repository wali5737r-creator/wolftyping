How WPM is calculated

The WPM shown is net WPM: the characters you typed correctly, divided by 5, divided by the minutes taken. Gross WPM, counting all typed characters, is also shown on the result screen. Accuracy is the share of typed characters that were correct, with skipped words counted as errors.

There is no single official "good" speed — it depends on what you are preparing for. WolfTyping shows your net WPM each time so you can track your own improvement.
